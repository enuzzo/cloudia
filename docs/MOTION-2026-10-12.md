## Rendering implementation

256³ macro density and cached light, 128³ fine detail and 384 nominal ray steps remain at full configured resolution. A 16³ flow field is stored as 64 half-float phases in a 16×16×1024 atlas (2 MiB). It is prepared once per seed, not uploaded each frame. Both pulse periods divide the 68-second loop; the upper undulation has the same period.

A small compute pass blends the two neighboring phases into a 16³ rgba16float field (32 KiB) before the cloud draw. Temporal interpolation is performed once per flow voxel instead of twice at every occupied ray sample. The shader samples this compact field once, then evaluates density, optical detail and cached lighting at the same deformed material coordinate. This keeps features and shadows registered, but **co-deformed static light is approximate**, especially during global growth; there is no dynamic exact shadow integration or simulated condensation/evaporation. Discrete time interpolation is position-continuous, with possible slope changes at phase boundaries.

Flow displacement is bounded by [.205,.58,.205] world units; spatial/temporal interpolation is convex. Occupancy alpha includes these bounds plus previous fine warp and macro interpolation. A three-axis prefix sum of difference cuboids constructs the larger support mask without an expensive per-neighbor dilation loop. During growth ordinary steps are retained; at 38 seconds reference space is identical to world space and the proven integer double stride resumes at Massima. Density is still integrated at ordinary steps. A seed-specific ray box also encloses raw linear-density support, fine warp and flow. It includes the growth anchors so all contracting phases fit, while texture coordinates keep their original global mapping. No hidden resolution reduction or temporal reconstruction was added.

Pause freezes formation and flow; reset returns to phase zero. One rAF chain remains. Seed regeneration refills the atlas and replaces density/light together. GPU flow resources and the compute node are disposed at teardown. GPU timestamps labelled **rendering** exclude the compute pass; CPU callback work includes compute and draw submission, and rAF cadence captures the overall callback intervals. Neither certifies compositor presentation.
