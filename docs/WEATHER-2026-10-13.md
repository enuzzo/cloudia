# Convection, atmosphere and storm — 13 October 2026

Local implementation on `codex/cumulonimbus-webgpu`, starting at `73c29a9`. No deployment, push, camera or gestures. Main-thread rendering remains the default; `?worker=1` remains optional. Commissioner and the legacy/hero engine are preserved.

## What is running

- Five rising local thermals, with different lifetimes (29.3, 36.47, 43.64, 50.81 and 57.98 simulation seconds), moving centers, radial expansion and peripheral downward roll. Their birth/retirement envelope is continuous. Upper outflow has separate spatially varying shear. The old 68-second atlas loop is no longer used by the live renderer. A 16³ GPU compute evaluates the analytic field each frame; it uploads no changing volume. The historical atlas helper remains a tested CPU reference.
- A fourth flow channel modulates local entrainment erosion. Per-axis saturation stays strictly within the existing [.205,.58,.205] occupancy displacement bound. Base motion remains anchored. Density/detail and cached optical depth use the same material coordinate. The original accelerated 38-second global formation is retained; this is authored evolution, not fluid simulation or a meteorological forecast.
- Ice fibres now separate more strongly from intermediate-density material; static lighting bake and shader share the new anisotropic erosion/strand model. The broad anvil shape still derives from the seeded envelope. Fine texture frequency and the optical cache still limit photographic detail.
- A 128² half-float sky table integrates single scattering through spherical Earth atmosphere with exponential Rayleigh/Mie layers (24 view samples, eight solar samples). It is prepared at startup or when sun/haze changes, not per frame. The cloud's direct solar color and ambient fill derive from the same atmosphere. Clear/haze/storm controls add explicit haze, storm attenuation and approximate aerial perspective. There is a low-order fill term and an approximate lower hemisphere; this is not a complete multiple-scattering or terrain solution.
- Intracloud sheet lightning: 3–5 unequal strokes with short attacks, irregular gaps and decay, distributed internal emission/scattering halo plus a directional sky spill. Both use the same scene-space discharge origin, including during early growth. There is no ground channel/branched bolt geometry in this checkpoint. `Isola luce del lampo` intentionally holds .85 intensity without producing thunder; `Lampo`, pause, reset or maturity releases it. Automatic flashes are suppressed during this held preview.
- Thunder runs on the DOM thread, even with the render worker. `Attiva audio` creates/resumes Web Audio in the user's click handler and prepares four ten-second stereo sources once. Distributed noise arrivals, low-frequency resonances, distance-dependent lowpass/gain and modest pan vary the result. Audio schedules from the first illuminated submitted frame, compensating worker-message delivery time. Sound delay is distance /343 m/s: 1/3/8 km → 2.92/8.75/23.32 s. This is real wall-clock time, independent of the cloud speed control. Distance is an acoustic audition control; it does not move the camera or establish a world-size calibration.
- Pause, reset, new cloud, maturity, isolated-flash preview, hidden tab, failure and destruction cancel pending/playing thunder. Mute suspends the context; teardown closes it. Audio synthesis and browser activation were exercised; speaker/headphone playback and acoustic realism still need owner listening. No microphone permission is requested.

## Research and choice

[NWS storm sequences](https://www.weather.gov/spotterguide/types) support local rising cells and the ordering of buildup and outflow; the reference photographs span minutes, whereas the demo formation is accelerated. [NWS thunder](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-science-thunder) explains delayed arrivals from different portions of the channel and the distinction between nearby cracks and distant rumbles. The procedural sound is an approximation of those cues.

[Guerrilla Nubis, Evolved (2022)](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/nubis-evolved) discusses internal volumetric flashes without temporal upscaling; [Nubis³ (2023)](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/nubis-cubed) discusses voxel modeling, lighting and acceleration. [Hillaire's atmosphere publications](https://sebh.github.io/publications/) establish atmosphere/lighting coherence and LUT approaches. Cloudia's small single-scattering integration is authored here, not a port of these production systems; no copyrighted renderer source was imported. The full EGSR2020 PDF could not be fetched by the browsing tool, so its uninspected details are not claimed as implemented.
