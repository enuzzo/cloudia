Prior modifications: partial overview4 crop, exact valid-aware rectangular-area
aggregation into2048x2048 cells62.5m; linear Float32 RGB conditional mean and
separate valid-area fraction. X west/Z north, domain128km around44N10E.

This further derivative: pack one IEEE754 binary16 little-endian RGBA texture
Q=(RGB*validity,validity), without spatial resampling, gain, gamma, new masks,
palette fitting or synthetic detail. No TIFF source re-read or new download.
The original Float32 RGB/validity fields remain unchanged in their archive.

The annual composite contains observed illumination/shadow and mixed dates;
no physical albedo/BRDF recovery, perfect de-lighting, certified cloud mask,
forest geometry, current-season representation or official endorsement is claimed.
The validity fraction is the previously frozen four-band DN gate, not optical
opacity or independent cloud truth. NIR is not present in this RGBA output.
