The actual-mesh CPU witness changes the shadow diagnosis: all three selected dark pixels AND all three unchanged neighbours have physical blockers in the current Float32 mesh. The neighbouring pixels are lit by the shadow-map reference despite those blockers. Affine-plane extrapolation across a whole light texel can subtract hundreds of metres at a grazing caster. No new bias is adopted. Offline clipping of the saved triangle to the texel makes all six blocked in this small sample, but all six lookup UVs lie outside the winning caster triangle. No physically unoccluded control exists in this witness; it cannot qualify a clipping implementation.
